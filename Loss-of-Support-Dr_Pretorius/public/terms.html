1. What this form is

A way to give Savage Jooste & Adams (“the firm”) the particulars of a person who died in a road accident, of everyone who depended on that person, and of the accident itself, so that an attorney can assess and administer a claim for loss of support. It is a convenience. You may give the same information by telephone, by email or in person, and nothing is lost by doing so.

2. It does not, by itself, make us your attorneys

Submitting this form does not create an attorney–client relationship and does not oblige the firm to accept your matter. A relationship begins only when the firm confirms in writing that it acts for you, on terms agreed with you.

Until then, do not assume the firm is acting on any deadline in your matter. Claims against the Road Accident Fund are subject to strict time limits, and those limits continue to run whether or not you have sent this form.

3. Nothing here is legal advice

The form collects information. It does not assess your claim, advise you on your prospects, or tell you what to do next. Any advice will come from an attorney, after the firm has taken on your matter.

4. What we ask of you

  • Give information that is true and complete as far as you know it. A claim can be damaged by an inaccuracy given in good faith just as easily as by a deliberate one.
  • List every dependant. A person left off this form may never have their claim brought at all. If you are unsure whether someone counts, put them in and say why — that is for the attorney to decide, not for you.
  • Submit only your own information, or information you are entitled to give — as a spouse, parent, guardian, executor, or someone acting under a power of attorney. By submitting you confirm that you are entitled to give the details of the deceased and of every dependant you have listed, and that you will tell any adult dependant that you have done so.
  • Upload only documents and photographs relevant to this claim, and only ones you are entitled to share.
  • Do not attempt to interfere with the form, submit automatically, or use it for anything other than its purpose.

5. Your saved progress file is yours to look after

“Save progress” downloads a file to your own device so you can continue later. That file contains everything you have entered — including the identity numbers of the deceased and of every dependant you have listed, and any document you have attached. It is not sent to the firm or stored anywhere by this website.

Keeping it safe, and deleting it when you no longer need it, is your responsibility. If you are using a shared or public computer, do not use this feature.
